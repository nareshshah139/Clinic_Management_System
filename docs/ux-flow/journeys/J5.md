**Journey**: `docs/ux-paths/topics/pharmacy-counter-billing.md` STORY-603, "Doctor prescribes, then the pharmacist reviews, bills, prints, collects and dispenses (end to end)". This is the core money path.

- **Test data**: I created the prescription for the new patient Ananya Varma (9866077889) through the API: Clindamycin 1% Gel 20g, Doxycycline 100 mg strip, Sunscreen SPF 50 50 ml. The story's products don't exist in the seed. The invoice was PHI-2026-005, total ₹990.08.

### Decision points (9; 6 needless)
1. **Doctor: "Go to Pharmacy?" Stay Here / Yes** (needless). The prescription goes into the pharmacy queue either way. The dialog makes the doctor choose a billing hand-off that isn't her job, and "Yes, go to Pharmacy" is the visually primary button (walker step-16c).
2. **Which of five equal "go to bill" buttons to press** (needless): header **Bill**, **Open bill** (Active Dispense), **Collect payment** (Pick + Pay, also styled as primary), the Rx-queue row **Bill**, and Billing's row **Load** (02-counter, 06-counter-bottom, 14-billing-scroll-00). All five open the same builder.
3. **Accept / Sub / Edit / NA per line** (valid). Accept is the 90% answer, but there is no "Accept all".
4. **Ready, then Open bill** (needless). Ready is a precondition click that adds nothing. "Open bill" could imply "ready". After Ready, the "Pick + Pay" checklist already ticks **GST bill** and **Barcode** although no bill exists (11-tall-ready).
5. **Quantity per pack-sold line** (valid but asked twice; see Re-entry).
6. **Payment Method** (valid, but its value has no effect). Choosing UPI doesn't record payment (DAT-04).
7. **Confirm Invoice (green) vs Confirm & Print (dark)** (needless as two equal primaries). Both are full-width, high-contrast buttons in the same panel (17-print-preview). One primary with a "print later" option would cover both.
8. **Copy Type Original/Duplicate** (valid, default is correct).
9. **Counter "Paid" vs Invoices "Mark as Paid"** (needless). These are two "paid" verbs in two places, and only the second one records money.

### Re-entry (3)
1. **Quantity is decided twice.** The Counter shows "IN STOCK Qty 30" for Clindamycin gel and "Pick 60 · 24 available" for sunscreen. The pharmacist **Accepts** those lines (07-tall, 10-tall). The bill then opens the same lines at **Qty 1** with "Sold per tube. Filled 1; confirm how many the course needs" (14-billing-scroll-02/04). The Counter's Accept didn't settle quantity, so the work happens again on the next screen.
2. **Payment is captured three times and recorded once.** It goes in as Payment Method "UPI" in the builder (step 14), then **Mark as Paid** in Invoices (steps 20–22), then **Paid** at the Counter (step 25). Only Mark as Paid changes `paymentStatus`. The Invoices row reads "Payment: UPI" next to a **PENDING** badge (23-invoices-list).
3. **Patient re-selection.** Returning to the Counter after Invoices needs the patient picked again (step 24). The Billing URL also re-carries `patientId`, `doctorId` and `prescriptionId` that the Counter already holds.

### Confirmations
- **Mark as Paid**: a native `confirm()` followed by a native `alert()`. Recording a payment is an undo-able bookkeeping action. The alert in particular guards nothing (step 22).
- **Confirm Invoice** is a legitimate guard. The invoice can't be voided (BRK-26), so the preview-then-confirm step earns its place.

### Dead ends (3)
1. **After Confirm & Print** the builder shows "This checkout is confirmed. Reopen it for another copy." with **Reopen confirmed invoice** and **Start a new invoice** (20-confirmed-banner). There is no "Record payment" and no "Back to Counter / Dispense", which are the two things that actually come next.
2. **After Mark as Paid** there is only an `alert()` on the Invoices list, with no route back to the Counter or the patient (24-after-mark-paid).
3. **After Dispense**, the Counter keeps **Collect payment** as the primary button and "SCAN NEXT · Pick 1 · Unmapped shelf" for a finished order. There is no "Next patient" action (26-tall-counter-dispensed).

### Hesitations (8)
- **H1**: At 1280x800 the Active Dispense review table (the lines with Accept/Sub/Edit/NA) renders at **0 px height**. The measured scroll container `div.min-h-0.divide-y.overflow-y-auto` reports h 0 and scrollHeight 284. Even at 1400 px tall it shows a 16 px strip (07-tall). The 8-state lifecycle "pill cloud" and a vertical "Reason or pharmacist note" textarea take its space (03, 07-tall). The first action of the journey isn't visible. *(Layout defect, see ux-visual PH-11; it shapes the flow because the user can't start.)*
- **H2**: The lifecycle chip renders one letter per line ("I n R e v i e w", "D i s p e n s e d") (03, 25).
- **H3**: "Qty 30" for a 20 g gel and "TABLET ONCE_DAILY" under a gel (07-tall, PH-28). This invites a wrong Accept.
- **H4**: After **Open bill**, the Billing page opens with another full "Prescription dispensing queue" table above the bill. The table still shows Ananya as **Pending**, although the Counter has just marked her Ready (14-billing-scroll-00). The user has to decide whether to press **Load**.
- **H5**: The bill's only primary, **Print Preview**, sits in the right column 600 px from the item cards. It is not obvious that "preview" is the step that commits the sale.
- **H6**: The Payment Method select implies payment is being taken. Nothing on the builder says the invoice will remain unpaid (16-billing-ready-to-preview).
- **H7**: **Mark as Paid** is an unlabeled green check icon among View, Download and Delete icons (23-invoices-list).
- **H8**: After the invoice is confirmed, the Counter already shows lifecycle **Dispensed** + **Invoice** and the queue chip **Done**, because the server derives the status from the invoice (25). The footer still says "Ready for billing review" with **Ready / Paid / Dispense / Open bill** enabled (j5i log). The user can't tell whether to click Paid and Dispense or whether the order is done.

*Flow-shaping functional note (one line)*: re-opening the same Open-bill URL in a fresh tab after confirming rebuilt a **new prefilled draft** (Qty 1, ₹872.48) with no "already billed" warning (21-billing-reload-confirmed-banner). That invites a duplicate sale (related: BRK-10, BRK-25).

## The Simpler Version

Same goal, same people, same legal and safety checks (the pharmacist still reviews each line and confirms the invoice).

1. **Doctor**: click **Create Prescription**. A toast says "Sent to pharmacy queue". There is no dialog.
2. **Pharmacist**: open **Pharmacy Counter**. The oldest waiting patient is preselected. Click the patient if it is someone else.
3. Review the lines in a full-width table. Each line shows the pack-based quantity already computed: "Clindamycin 1% gel 20 g × **1 tube** — course needs ≥1; adjust?", or "Doxycycline 100 mg × 3 strips (30 tabs)". Adjust the one ambiguous line inline (Clindamycin → 2).
4. Click **Accept all & bill**. This one action marks the lines reviewed and opens the bill as a panel in the same workspace, without a page change. Exceptions (Sub, NA) stay per-line.
5. The bill is prefilled from step 3 (quantities, GST, directions). Choose payment **UPI**. A "Payment received" checkbox defaults to checked.
6. Click **Confirm ₹990.08 & print**. The invoice is confirmed, the payment is recorded (status PAID), and print opens.
7. The Counter shows "Hand over: 3 items" with **Dispensed**. One click records dispensing and auto-selects the next patient.

That is 7 numbered steps and about 9 interactions: Create Rx, Counter, select, adjust qty, Accept all & bill, payment method (2 clicks), Confirm & print, Dispensed. **This saves about 18 interactions, removes 3 page changes and 2 native dialogs, and closes the unpaid-sale gap.**

### Concrete changes
1. **Remove the "Go to Pharmacy?" dialog** after Create Prescription. Use a toast with an optional "Open in pharmacy" link for doctors who self-dispense.
2. **Record payment at checkout.** Add "Payment received" (default on) with amount-tendered fields to Billing Information. When it is on, the checkout records the payment. "Mark as Paid" stays in Invoices for credit or late payers only (fixes DAT-04).
3. **Merge the Counter's review and the bill's quantity review.** Compute pack quantities once, at review time, and carry them into the bill. Delete the bill's "Filled 1; confirm" banners for lines already accepted at the Counter.
4. **Add "Accept all"** to the review table and **fold Ready into it**: Accept all & bill. Drop the separate **Ready** and **Paid** task buttons. Paid derives from the invoice, and Dispensed is the only manual post-bill click.
5. **One "go to bill" entry point.** Keep one context-aware primary in Active Dispense (Review → "Accept all & bill"; billed and unpaid → "Record payment"; paid → "Mark dispensed"). Remove the header **Bill**, **Collect payment**, the Rx-queue row **Bill**, and the Billing-page **Load** table when arriving with a `prescriptionId`.
6. **Open the bill in place** (side panel or drawer) instead of navigating to `?section=billing` under a second queue table. Failing that, hide the queue table whenever a prescription is loaded.
7. **Free space for the review table**: replace the lifecycle pill cloud with one chip, merge Pick Assist and Scan Next into the table rows, and drop the checklist-as-buttons.
8. **One confirm button in the preview** ("Confirm & print"), with "Confirm without printing" as a link. Drop the duplicated Invoice Controls facts.
9. **Terminal screens offer the next step.** After confirm: "Back to Counter — mark dispensed". After dispensing: auto-advance to the next queued patient.
10. **Remove the duplicate directions block** on each bill card (keep one editable, collapsed block).
11. **Guard re-billing**: when a prescription already has a confirmed invoice, Open bill should show that invoice ("Already billed PHI-2026-005 — reprint / view"), not a new draft.
