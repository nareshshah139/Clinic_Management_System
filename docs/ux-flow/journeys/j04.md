## Which route does the screen push?

The Dashboard (`01-dashboard.png`) has nothing for a pharmacist: user counts, "System Alerts", and no prescription queue or pharmacy shortcut (measured). The only way in is the sidebar. Its Pharmacy group is ordered Pharmacy Desk, Pharmacy Counter, Sales (`01-dashboard.png`).

| C. Sales | sidebar Sales, "New sale", "Load" on the queue at the top of the bill page, pay, preview, confirm, close | 8 | suspected (not replayed) |

Why A: the Counter opens with Meera already selected (dark card, "Queued", `02-counter-cockpit.png`) and a dark "Open bill" button next to her; no search and no row click are needed. The Desk (first in the menu) shows metrics, Partner Daily Sync and Treatment Packages, and no queue; its "Pharmacy Counter" tile is just a second door to A. Sales has the register first, which is useless for a waiting prescription. The same prescription appears in the Counter "Queue" panel, "Active Dispense", the "Prescription Queue" table under it, and the queue repeated on the bill page. Three buttons on the Counter all do the same thing: header "Bill" (`PharmacyCounterCockpit.tsx:597`), "Open bill" (`:952`), "Collect payment" (`:1230`). Together with Desk "New sale" and Sales "New sale" that is five ways into one bill screen.

## 4. Clarity Issues

- "Pharmacy Desk", "Pharmacy Counter" and "Sales" are three menu items for one job; "Bill", "Open bill", "Collect payment", "New sale", "Load" are five names for starting a bill (`PharmacyCounterCockpit.tsx:597,952,1230`, `pharmacy-workspace.tsx:339`).
- The bill page header says "Sales" with a "Back to Sales" button although the pharmacist came from the Counter; the title "New bill" is also wrong for a prescription ("Bill for Meera Shah").
- "Confirm & Print" confirms the sale and moves stock; the word "Print" hides that a payment is being recorded as UPI. "Confirm Invoice" and "Confirm & Print" are two names for one action.
- "Print Preview" on a button that actually starts the commit flow is misleading: the user presses "Print Preview" to pay.
- "Prescription ID: cuxrxwaiting..." is a system id, meaningless to staff.
- "Exceptions" counts and "Queue 6" mix done and waiting prescriptions in one list (`02-counter-cockpit.png`, measured: Done rows listed next to the one Queued row).
- Payment Method option "Net Banking" vs register "Net banking" (story file, suspected).

## 7. Verdict

**Acceptable.** The prefill is excellent (0 re-entry, 3 lines, doctor, name and phone arrive ready) and the committing step is guarded once. The journey costs 7 actions against 4 ideal: a two-click payment select, a "Print Preview" stop that saves nothing, and a Close. The bigger problem is around the journey: a dashboard with nothing for the pharmacist, three overlapping menu items, five ways to start the same bill, and a Counter cockpit full of review controls the bill does not read.
