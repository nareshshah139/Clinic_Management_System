About 11 actions are prescribing work. The rest are navigation, scrolling, an interrupting dialog, duplicate saves and a handoff the doctor should not have to perform. The walker logged 17 steps to the handoff, with the visit "still not completed".

### Decisions: 5 needless [measured]

1. The **Doctor selector** on Appointments (same as J3).
2. **"Go to Pharmacy?" Stay / Go** after Create. The persona has to pick Stay, because Go skips printing (story Variation "Dialog route").
3. **Which handoff route**: the footer "Go to Pharmacy" (passes `visitId`, does not save) or the dialog "Yes, go to Pharmacy" (passes `prescriptionId`). They produce different results (F-1-01), and the user cannot see why.
4. **Which save**: "Create Prescription" (footer), "Save Draft" (footer), "Save Prescription" (green, in the preview), or Print, which also saves (walk step 15). That is four ways to persist the same Rx.
5. **Print format, chosen twice**: the footer "Print: Table" select and the preview sidebar "Print Format: Table" (`shot:step-11-create-dialog.png`, `step-13-print-preview.png`). Letterhead, margins and page breaks are also re-offered on every print, where a remembered doctor default would do.

### Re-entry: 3 [measured]

1. The doctor re-selects herself in the Doctor selector.
2. **The template drugs are not linked to the drug master.** The builder shows "Stock: —", and at Pharmacy Billing someone must "Link item" for Adapalene and Benzoyl Peroxide (F-1-05, `shot:step-04b-template-applied.png`, `step-17-pharmacy-handoff.png`). The clinic re-identifies drugs the template already named.
3. **Progress is lost after Back** (F-1-06). Section completions the app already holds (a saved Rx) have to be re-triggered before Complete is allowed.

### Confirmations: 2 that guard nothing [measured]

- The **"Go to Pharmacy?"** modal is a navigation offer shown as a blocking dialog.
- The browser **"Leave site?"** prompt on footer "Go to Pharmacy" (walk step 17). It warns about unsaved data that the button itself chose not to save. The right fix is to save, not to confirm.

### Dead ends: 3 [measured]

1. The **dashboard "Today's Appointments"** list is visible but not actionable. It also shows the raw "IN_PROGRESS" status (F-1-09).
2. **Pharmacy Billing on the doctor's screen.** A DOCTOR has no Pharmacy links in the sidebar, the page is a pharmacist's work queue and invoice builder, and the only way back is browser Back (STORY-008 Variation, `shot:step-17-pharmacy-handoff.png`).
3. **Complete Visit after returning** stays disabled, because progress is 0% (`shot:step-18-back-to-visit.png`). The journey's final step cannot be reached.

### Hesitations [measured]

- Where are the medicines? The table starts 3 viewports down, at y=2524.
- "Total qty: 0" with 28 tablets prescribed (F-1-08).
- The "When" column reads "Aft/er/Lu/nc/h", and frequency shows "ONCE_/DAILY" (F-1-07).
- The frequency header says "(0-1-0)" while the template rows show "ONCE_DAILY".
- In the preview, three equally heavy buttons compete: Save Prescription, Print and Download PDF (F-1-11).
- "Refill eligible" is printed although no refill was ever set (F-1-02).
- The leave-site prompt fires on a button labelled as a handoff.

## Take-away Pass

### Builder top: template bar (`shot:step-04a-templates-open.png`)

- **Templates** dropdown: keep it, it is the step that saves the most work.
- **Refresh**: it reloads the template list. Remove it, and refetch on open.
- **Undo / Redo**: keep them as icons or Ctrl+Z. They are not text buttons in a primary toolbar.
- **"Reset to default"**: bright red, the loudest element on the tab (VIS-13). Put it in a "Template ▾" menu.
- **"Save current" / "Save fields" / "New template"**: three save-as-template verbs. Make them one "Save as template…" entry in the same menu.
- A template **silently injects a chief complaint** ("Acne lesions on face"), which the doctor never typed but which prints (walk step 13, `shot:step-13-print-preview.png`). Make the injected text visibly highlighted in the form, or have templates fill medicines only.

### Post-create modal (`shot:step-11-create-dialog.png`)

Remove it entirely. It interrupts every save, including re-saves (story Variation "Re-saving"), and its "Go" branch skips printing.

### Print preview (`shot:step-13-print-preview.png`, `step-14-refill-stamp.png`)

**Sidebar**: before the first action button the doctor scrolls past about 15 controls:
- the Print Settings Tip (emoji, browser instructions)
- Live Preview: Off
- Zoom
- Top margin and Bottom margin
- Reset margins
- Space-optimized layout
- Line spacing
- Print Format (duplicates the footer select)
- Letterhead
- 4 Page-break checkboxes and "Avoid breaks inside tables"
- Printer profile (always empty: dead UI, F-1-03)
- Refill stamp (prints a false claim, F-1-02)
- Show signature

All of these are per-doctor layout preferences that change rarely. Recommendation:
- Move them into one collapsed **"Layout ▾"** panel, or into doctor settings, which already partly hold them (Customization tab, My Settings).
- Remember them per doctor. The signature already works this way: "Remembered for this doctor on this browser".
- Remove Printer profile until it can be created.
- Gate Refill stamp on refills existing.

**Header chips**: "Rx: 3", "Investigations: 0" and "Follow-up: Yes" restate the page right next to them. Remove them.

**Action buttons**: about 9 buttons (VIS-07):
- green **Save Prescription**
- Close (×2, plus the dialog X)
- Interactions
- Email
- PDF via WhatsApp
- High contrast
- **Print** and **Download PDF** (both solid dark)

Make **Print** the single primary. Remove Save Prescription, because Print already saves first (walk step 15). Make Download PDF, Email and WhatsApp a secondary "Share ▾" group. Keep one Close. Remove High contrast from the preview: it is a display-accessibility setting that belongs in app settings, and it leaks app-wide anyway (F-1-04).

### Handoff (`shot:step-17-pharmacy-handoff.png`)

- Taking the doctor into Pharmacy Billing (a prescription queue, then "Linked Prescription" with "Link item" tasks, then "New Pharmacy Invoice with Packages") shows her a pharmacist's work surface.
- The Rx is already in that queue the moment it is saved. STORY-008 has reception "Load" it independently.
- **Remove both doctor-side "Go to Pharmacy" routes.** Replace them with a status line on the Rx ("In pharmacy queue · not yet dispensed"). That makes "hand off" a zero-click step.
- If a clinic wants the doctor to bill, keep a single secondary "Open in Pharmacy Billing" link that saves first and passes `prescriptionId`.

## The Simpler Version

The same goal in 8 steps, Complete Visit included. The story's ideal of 8 excludes Complete.

1. **Dashboard "Today" → click Arjun's row.** It defaults to the signed-in doctor. The visit opens **on the Rx section**, because the doctor role has no pending Vitals or Photos work and the nurse took vitals.
2. **Templates ▾ → "Acne (Mild)".** It fills only the medicines, linked to drug-master items with stock shown.
3. **Diagnosis** "Acne vulgaris – grade 2" (field directly above the medicine table).
4. **Add medicine → "doxy" → pick**, then the inline presets on that row: **0-1-0 · After food · 4w**. Instructions are typed in the row.
5. **Review "4w" shortcut** directly under the table. It fills the review date and follow-up text.
6. **Print** (the primary action, always visible). It saves the Rx, which puts it in the pharmacy queue, and opens the preview with the remembered letterhead and signature. The preview shows the page, plus Print and a "Share ▾" menu.
7. **Print** in the browser dialog. Hand Arjun the paper: "Pharmacy counter, please."
8. **Complete Visit**. It is enabled, because diagnosis plus Rx meets the required-fields gate. The screen offers "Next patient".

## What is already good

- **Medicine search**: type-ahead with Best Match, stock counts in red at ≤5, and regimen defaults on pick (`shot:step-07-doxy-search.png`).
- **Dose and duration presets** are one click each.
- **The template dropdown** is clearly grouped ("Default templates").
- **Print saves first**, so the printout always matches the record.
- **The "Show signature" disabled state explains itself** ("No signature uploaded. The doctor can add one in My Settings → Doctor signature").
- **The printed sheet is clean**: patient block, diagnosis, Rx table and follow-up (VIS "Excellent as is").
- **Vitals carry over from the Vitals tab** without re-typing.
