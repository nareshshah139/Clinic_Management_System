### Decisions: 4 needless [measured]

1. **Doctor selector** on Appointments. 100% of signed-in doctors want themselves, so this should be a default.
2. **"Go to Pharmacy?" Stay / Go** after every Create Prescription. The doctor documenting a visit picks "Stay" (this story and J4 both did).
3. **Which save to press.** "Mark Vitals Complete", "Create Prescription", "Save Labs", "Save Draft" and Ctrl/Cmd+S autosave all exist. The doctor has to learn which one moves the progress gate.
4. **Tag dialog Mode Grid/Single and "Infer positions".** Inference was already correct (walker step 10–11). The only needed action is Upload. This is mild: the mode toggle is harmless, but it competes with an Upload button that is off-screen.

Consultation type ("In-person") is already defaulted. That one is fine.

### Re-entry: 2 [measured]

1. **The doctor re-selects herself** in the Appointments Doctor selector (step 2).
2. **The diagnosis is entered twice**: the free-text "Diagnosis" field (Prescription tab y=1192) and the "Dermatology Diagnoses" chip under On Examination. Both are needed in the story (steps 15 and 16 of the walk).
3. Not counted: vitals appear again in Prescription → "Clinical Details & Vitals" but are synced, so nothing is re-typed. This is duplicated *display* only (`shot:step-13-prescription-tab.png`).

### Confirmations: 1 that guards nothing [measured]

- The "Go to Pharmacy?" modal (`shot:step-16c-create-rx-dialog.png`) is a navigation offer shown as a blocking dialog. Nothing destructive is guarded.

### Dead ends: 3 [measured]

1. **Calendar without the evening appointment.** Today's 18:30–19:45 tiles are not rendered (F-401-01, APT-01). This is a functional defect, noted here because it removes the journey's entry point. The dashboard "Today's Appointments" rows are not clickable either (F-1-09), so there is no other UI route.
2. **Disabled Complete Visit** with no reason. Its toast says "Fill vitals, complaints, diagnosis, and prescription", and all of those are already done (`shot:step-17-complete-blocked.png`).
3. **After completion**, the screen offers no "next patient" or "back to today" action. The tracker shows "Continue / View / Bill", the header still says "Unsaved changes", and the form stays fully editable (`shot:step-21-completed.png`, F-401-13). The only way out is the generic "Back to Calendar" in the header.

### Hesitations [measured unless marked]

- The Doctor selector shows someone else (step 2).
- START, INFO and RESCHED appear only on hover, on an 18px tile (APT-12).
- The first form field sits below the fold, because the header block takes 0–600px (`shot:step-04-form-header-ananya.png`).
- "Mark Vitals Complete" gives the toast "Visit saved" but no green dot (`shot:step-08-vitals-saved.png`).
- Upload is invisible in the tag dialog.
- Complaints and diagnosis are not under a tab with a matching name. They sit under **Prescription**, and a first-time user would look for a "Notes" or "Assessment" tab. [suspected: the label mismatch is measured, the stall itself is inferred]
- Create Prescription and Complete Visit are clipped off the right edge.
- Complete is disabled with a misleading message.
- The "Δ +18" lab delta implies a previous value of 0 (F-401-11).

### Tabs: Overview · Vitals · Photos · Prescription · Lab Tests · Customization · History

- **Overview** holds Visit Information, Progress Summary and Quick Actions (`shot:step-12-overview-photos-captured-0.png`).
  - Visit Information repeats header facts, and its photo count is wrong (0 vs 3).
  - Progress Summary is the 4th progress display.
  - Quick Actions ("Record Vitals", "Capture Photos") duplicate the tabs one row above.
  - **Remove the tab.** Open the visit on the first section with work to do.
- **Customization** holds print margins, grayscale and letterhead (STORY-401 Variations). This is a per-doctor print setting, not visit documentation, and it is also duplicated by the print-preview sidebar sliders and My Settings (`prescriptions-print-share.md` line 7). **Move it out of the visit.**
- **History** ("Patient Visit History", `shot:step-23-history.png`) duplicates Patient Context → "Recent Visits", which is also wrong ("No diagnosis"). Keep one: the History content belongs in the Patient Context column as an expandable list.
- That leaves **Vitals · Photos · Notes & Rx · Labs**: 4 tabs instead of 7. Or use one scrolling page with anchors (see the Simpler Version).

### Vitals tab (`shot:step-07c-vitals-filled.png`)

- **Mark Vitals Complete**: remove it. Autosave already runs once a visit exists, and completion should come from data ("BP entered" means Vitals is done).
- "Pain Score (0-10)": keep it, because it is optional and small.
- The SpO₂ input is missing (story step 7). This is a coverage gap, not a simplification point.

### Photos tab and tag dialog (`shot:step-09-photos-empty.png`, `step-10-tag-dialog.png`)

- The header "Upload" / "Camera" buttons duplicate the empty-state "Choose Files" / "Take Photo" buttons (F-401-18). Keep one pair: the header pair when photos exist, the empty-state pair when none do.
- The dialog shows a full-height preview, with the position Select and Upload below the fold. Pin the footer: Upload stays visible, and the position chips go under each thumbnail. Grid mode should be the default, so all 3 positions are checked at a glance and the doctor clicks Upload once.
- The red "Delete" button is the most saturated element in the Photos header (F-401-18). Demote it to an icon on the selected thumbnail.

### Prescription tab: in practice the entire clinical note [measured live]

- `main` scrollHeight is **4,727px**, about 5.9 viewports. It shows **107 visible buttons**.
- Section order and y-positions:

  | Section | y |
  |---|---|
  | Clinical Details & Vitals | 722 |
  | Chief Complaints | 916 |
  | Diagnosis | 1192 |
  | Patient History | 1378 |
  | On Examination | 1778 |
  | Treatment (medicine table) | 2524 |
  | Procedures | 3297 |
  | Investigations | 3606 |
  | Basic Information | 4156 |
  | Footer: Create Prescription, Go to Pharmacy, Save Draft, Complete Visit | below Basic Information |

- **"Clinical Details & Vitals"** repeats the Vitals tab (VIS-11). It uses different labels: "Pulse (bpm)" vs "Heart Rate", "BP (Sys)" vs "BP Systolic". Show it read-only as one line ("BP 112/74 · P 78 · 54 kg · BMI 21.6"), or drop it and keep the Vitals tab as the only editor.
- **Diagnosis free text plus "Dermatology Diagnoses" chips** record the same fact in two places, about 1,100px apart. Merge them into one Diagnosis field whose chips insert into the text.
- **Patient History** (Past / Medication / Menstrual / Family) sits between Diagnosis and Exam. On a follow-up it is already known from earlier visits. Collapse it by default, prefill it from the last visit, and move it above Chief Complaints or into Patient Context.
- **Investigations** section vs the **Lab Tests** tab: the same 30-test list appears in two places (walker cross-screen duplication). Keep one: order tests here, next to the Rx, and record results in Labs only when they exist.
- **Template toolbar**:
  - It has 8 controls: Templates, Refresh, Undo, Redo, red "Reset to default", "Save current", "Save fields" and "New template" (`shot:finding-prescription-tab-hoverflow.png`).
  - The bright red "Reset to default" is the loudest element on the tab (VIS-13).
  - Keep Templates. Put Undo/Redo behind keyboard shortcuts or icons, and put the three save-as-template verbs plus Reset in one "Template ▾" menu.
- **Treatment Suggestions card**: when there is no match it is the heaviest element ("No strong pattern yet", VIS-14). Collapse it to one line until it has a suggestion.
- **Footer**:
  - It has 6 actions across 2 rows: Print select, Print Preview, Create Prescription, Go to Pharmacy, Save Draft and Complete Visit (`shot:step-17-complete-blocked.png`).
  - **Create Prescription** and **Complete Visit** both look primary-ish. Create is solid dark, and Complete is disabled grey.
  - There should be exactly one primary: Complete Visit. It should save the Rx as part of completing.

### Lab Tests tab (`shot:step-18-labs-selected.png`, `step-20-labs-saved.png`)

- "Compare with previous visit" checkbox: remove it as a control. Show the delta automatically when a previous value exists, and nothing when it does not.
- "Save Labs" is the 6th save verb. Remove it (autosave).
- The photo-autofill native file input ("Choose File No file chosen") is an advanced feature. Put it behind an "Import from report photo" link.
- Copy: "Selections and results are saved under Treatment Plan → Dermatology." This names a location the doctor never sees. Remove it.

### Completed state (`shot:step-21-completed.png`)

- The tracker's "Continue / View / Bill", the "Unsaved changes" pill and the still-editable form all suggest the visit is not finished.
- Replace them with a completion banner, "Visit completed 11:52 · Rx sent to pharmacy", plus two actions: **Next patient: \<name\>** (primary) and **Back to today** (secondary).

## The Simpler Version

The same goal in 9 steps, 8 if no labs are ordered. Every input that carries clinical content is kept.

1. **Dashboard → "Today" list → click Ananya's 11:30 row** (or her calendar tile, which is wholly clickable). The calendar and the list default to the signed-in doctor, and the visit opens immediately.
2. The visit opens on a single scrolling page: compact header (name, age, allergies, "11:30 OPD", status) and section anchors **Vitals · Photos · Notes · Rx · Labs** pinned at the top. **Enter vitals.** Autosave creates the visit on the first keystroke, and there is no "Mark complete".
3. **Drop or choose 3 photos.** Positions are inferred in a grid with the Upload button pinned, so the step is one click to confirm.
4. **Chief complaint**: text plus concern chips. Patient History is collapsed and prefilled from the last visit.
5. **Diagnosis**: one field. Chips such as "Acne vulgaris" insert into it.
6. **Examination**: morphology and distribution chips plus detailed text.
7. **Rx**: search and add medicines, then presets. The table fits the column.
8. *(optional)* **Order labs**: tick Vitamin D and CBC in the single Investigations list. Results are entered later, when they exist.
9. **Complete Visit**, the one primary action, always visible in a sticky footer. It saves the Rx, puts it in the pharmacy queue without a modal, completes the appointment, and shows "Next patient".

| # | Change | Removes | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | **Delete the per-section save verbs** (Mark Vitals Complete, Save Labs, Create Prescription inside the visit, Save Draft button) in favour of autosave plus one Complete Visit. Section dots come from saved data. | Steps 7, 8, 18 and 24. Decision 3. Also fixes the J4 progress-reset problem by design. | M |

| 6 | **Restructure the tabs**: remove Overview (duplicate info and quick actions), move Customization to doctor settings, and fold History into Patient Context. Rename "Prescription" to **"Notes & Rx"**, or split Notes from Rx. | 3 tabs, the 4th progress display, the wrong "Photos Captured" | M |

- Vitals sync into the Rx section, with no re-typing.
