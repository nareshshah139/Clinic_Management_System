## 4. Clarity Issues

- "Create bill and record ₹400" is two verbs in one button. A desk person thinks "take ₹400". Better: "Take ₹400 cash" (the mode is in the label so a wrong mode is visible).
- "Record payment" heading and "Record ₹1,450 payment" button, "Amount received" label: consistent, but "Record" is back-office wording; "Take payment" matches how the desk speaks. Suspected preference, not measured.
- "Review" (chip, dialog) versus "Review consultation" (bill line) versus "First" (row short form) versus "First consultation": same concept, three spellings (`appointment-billing-view.ts` `BILL_TYPE_LABEL`, `BILL_TYPE_SHORT`, `defaultBillLine`).
- "Bill" versus "Invoice": the dialog says "Bill for", the toast says "Bill INV-...", the invoice number is "INV-...", the pharmacy side says "Invoices". Pick one.
- Procedure row says "Chemical Peel" on the line while the chip says "Procedure": fine.
- "Net banking / NEFT" button wraps and forces "Cheque" onto a second line (`06`), so mode choices look uneven.
- The Close control is an X with no label; a person who just finished does not see a "Done".

## 7. Verdict

**Acceptable.** The core of the journey is already close to minimal: every field the receptionist could have typed (bill line, fee, paid-now amount, amount due, mode) is prefilled correctly, and each leg is one chip click plus one save button. The two extra actions are the dialog that never closes itself, plus the receptionist reaching the calendar through a cluttered Appointments page; the dead end after saving (no receipt, no next patient) is the clarity gap. Separately, the stock seed cannot complete the Rohan leg (non-CUID invoice id), which is a seed defect, not a UI defect.
