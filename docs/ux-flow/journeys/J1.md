# J1: Receptionist books a routine OPD consultation for a returning patient

**Story**: `docs/ux-paths/topics/appointments-scheduling-rooms.md` STORY-001 (medium). Persona: Riya Sharma (RECEPTION), at the front desk all day.
**Goal**: Book a returning patient with Dr. Shravya at 11:30 for a 30-minute OPD consultation (Consultation Room 1 optional).
**Story ideal path**: 5 steps. Choose the doctor, type a few letters, pick the match, click the free 11:30 tile, confirm.
**Evidence**:
- Live traversal by this critic on 2026-11-07 at 1280x800 (Playwright, reception@clinic.test). Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Clinic-Management-System/1eff4a85-453f-5886-a484-208393c84f41/scratchpad/ux-flow/J1-screens/` and are cited as `J1:<file>`. Script: `…/scratchpad/ux-flow/scripts/j1.js`.
- Deviations: the seed has no Sneha Reddy, so the patient was **Kavya Patel (9876543213)**. The run was at 18:37 IST, after Dr. Shravya's 09:00–18:00 hours, so the booking was made for **09 Nov 2026 11:30**. Setting the date was an extra step caused by the clock, not by the design, and is **not** counted below.
- Related walker run: `docs/ux-walker/stories/appointments-scheduling-rooms--STORY-004/walk-report.md` (the same grid and dialog, reached from the patient chart).
- Visual findings: `docs/ux-visual/pages/appointments-and-visits-findings.md` (APT-xx).
**Verdict**: **convoluted** by the rubric. The step overhead is modest (7 needed steps vs 5 ideal). The verdict comes from the Appointments screen: it fails 4 screen heuristics (8, 9, 10, 11), it forces an order (patient first, then slot) with a dead end for the opposite order, and it offers a duplicate surface (Calendar vs Slots tabs).

Labels: **[measured]** means counted live or read from a screenshot or the code. **[suspected]** means a live check is still needed. **[story]** means taken from the catalog story and not re-measured.

### Re-entry: 0 on this route [measured]
The app does not ask for anything it already knows. The other route to the same goal does: the chart's "Book appointment" link drops the patient, so the patient is typed again (BRK-02, walker STORY-004: 9 actual vs 4 ideal).

### Confirmations
The **Book Appointment** dialog is a confirm step, and it earns its place. It carries the duration, type and room, its summary block (Patient / Phone / Date / Time Slot) is the best part of the flow (`J1:10`), and a booking can send a patient-facing WhatsApp. **Keep it, but make it fit without scrolling.** No needless confirmation was found.

### Dead ends: 1 hard, 1 soft [measured]
1. **Slot before patient.** Clicking a free tile with no patient selected shows the red toast "Patient required — Search and select a patient before booking a slot." in the top-right corner. Nothing opens. At that moment the Patient field is scrolled out of view and is not focused (`J1:06-slot-before-patient.png`, APT-09). The grid's own description says "Click an empty slot to schedule." (`J1:05`), which invites this exact order.
2. **Soft: after booking.** The green banner offers no next action (no "View", "Start visit", "Undo" or "Book another") and disappears after 5 s. The patient stays selected (`AppointmentsCalendar.tsx` `handleBookingConfirm` never clears `selectedPatientId`), so the next click on a free tile books **the same patient again** unless she presses **Clear** first [measured in code; the double-booking click itself was not exercised].

### Hesitations
- **Where do I start?** The page has no primary action. The only call to action is "click a tile", and empty tiles look like plain table rows (`J1:09`). The visually heaviest blocks above the grid are the Doctor/Date card, the tabs, the filter row and an 8-swatch, 2-line **Legend** (`J1:05`).
- **Is "Patient" a filter?** It sits between the "Room" filter and the "Grid" select and looks like them, so it reads as "show only this patient's appointments", not "who am I booking" (`J1:05`, APT-07).
- **Which row is 11:30?** Only whole hours are labelled (`J1:09`).
- **Tile vs dialog time.** The tile tooltip reads "Book 11:30-11:45" on a 15-minute grid, but the dialog shows 11:30-12:00 [story steps 6–7; tooltip logic in `DoctorDayCalendar.tsx:645-650`].
- **Date format.** The Date field shows `11/09/2026` (US mm/dd), while the dialog says "09 Nov 2026" (`J1:05` vs `J1:10`). In an Indian clinic, `11/09` reads as 11 September.

## The Simpler Version

Same goal, same information, fewest steps. The doctor is remembered and the date defaults to today, as now.

1. On **Appointments**, the grid is in the first viewport, with half-hour labels and a "+ Book" hover affordance on free rows. She clicks the free **11:30** row.
2. A booking popover opens beside the tile. The **Patient** field is focused. She types `sne`.
3. She picks "Sneha Reddy — 9849012345" (or presses Enter for the top match).
4. The popover shows one compact line, "OPD · 30 min · Room: Any available (change)", plus the patient/date/time summary. She clicks **Book** (or presses Enter).

**4 steps** (5 if she must switch doctor), against today's 7–8. Patient-first stays possible. Arriving with a patient already chosen (from the chart's "Book appointment" or a prior search) opens the popover pre-filled, so it takes 2 steps: click the tile, then Book.

### Concrete changes
1. **Let the tile click start the booking.** A free tile with no patient opens the booking dialog with patient search inside it, focused, instead of the "Patient required" toast. Move the Patient search out of the filter row and into the dialog (or keep a patient-first entry at the top of the page). This removes the dead end, the far-away field and one hesitation. *Effort M.*
2. **Make the dialog fit.** Use a sticky footer for Cancel/Confirm. For OPD, replace the room cards with a compact optional select (default "Any available"). Keep cards only for Procedure. This removes the in-dialog scroll and a needless decision. *Effort S.*
3. **Bring the grid up.** Remove the empty band. Merge the Doctor/Date card and the filter row into one toolbar. Collapse Visit Type, Room, Grid and Legend into "View options". This removes the page scroll. *Effort S.*
4. **Merge the Calendar and Slots tabs** into one grid (APT-08). This removes a needless decision and a duplicate surface. *Effort M.*
5. **Give the result an onward path.** The banner offers "View · Start visit · Undo". Booking clears the selected patient, or the badge says "Next booking: Kavya Patel — Clear". *Effort S.*
6. **Label the grid.** Show half-hour labels, or a time hint on hover such as "+ 11:30". *Effort S.*
7. Show each fact once: drop the duplicate patient echo (keep the badge) and the duplicate availability text on room cards. *Effort S.*

## Clarity Issues
- **"Click an empty slot to schedule."** (`J1:05`) is false until a patient is chosen. The toast that follows says "Search and select a patient before booking a slot." (`J1:06`). The same rule has a third wording in `AppointmentsCalendar.tsx:300`, "Select an existing patient or add a new one before booking.", and a fourth in the amber hint (`J1:07`). That is four phrasings of one rule.
- **"Patient" in a filter row** reads as a filter, and the placeholder is truncated ("Search patient by na"). A label such as "Book for patient" says what it does.
- **Raw codes in the dialog**: "Room Availability for OPD", "Room selection is optional for **opd** appointments." (lower-case enum), and "PROCEDURE *" on the procedure variant (APT-20). Plain words work better: "Room (optional)".
- **Date format drift**: `11/09/2026` in the Date field vs "09 Nov 2026" in the dialog (`J1:05`, `J1:10`). The US order is ambiguous in an Indian clinic.
- **Tile vs dialog duration**: "Book 11:30-11:45" on a 15-minute grid, then 11:30-12:00 in the dialog [story].
- **Legend jargon**: "Past (OPD)" and "Past (Procedure)" in near-identical pale tints, plus "Booking In Progress" as a permanent legend entry for a state that lasts under a second (`J1:05`, `J1:13`).
- **Abbreviated span actions** INFO / RESCHED / START appear on hover only (APT-12). They are not part of this journey, but they are the only way to act on the booking she just made.
- **Doctor naming**: the Doctor select says "Shravya Dermatologist", the walk-in form says "Dr. Shravya Dermatologist", and the dashboard has no doctor column (D08). The "Dr." prefix is inconsistent across surfaces.

### Functional bugs that shape this flow (one line each, already in defects.md)
- BRK-02: the chart's "Book appointment" deep link loses the patient, so the second route to this goal does not work.
- BRK-14: the Visit Type and Room filters make booked tiles look free, so the filters next to the booking input are a trap.
- DAT-14: the duration is stale on drag-select, so the drag variant books the wrong length.
