# J2: Walk-in patient registered and booked (two routes to one goal)

**Stories**:
- **Route A**: `docs/ux-paths/topics/patients-records.md` STORY-203, "Walk-in patient registered and auto-booked into the next free slot" (Patients → New Patient → Patient Type "Walk-in" → Create). Story ideal: **5**.
- **Route B**: `docs/ux-paths/topics/appointments-scheduling-rooms.md` STORY-002, "Walk-in first-time patient created inline while booking" (Appointments → Patient search → Add New Patient → slot → Confirm). Story ideal: **6**.

Persona: Riya (RECEPTION) at a busy desk. **Goal**: a first-time walk-in gets a patient record and a slot with the right doctor as soon as possible.

**Evidence**:
- Live traversal by this critic on 2026-11-07 at 1280x800 (Playwright, reception@clinic.test). Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Clinic-Management-System/1eff4a85-453f-5886-a484-208393c84f41/scratchpad/ux-flow/J2-screens/` and are cited as `J2:<file>` (`A-` = route A, `B-` = route B, `P-` = typing probe). Scripts: `…/scratchpad/ux-flow/scripts/j2.js` (`A` / `B`) and `j2-probe-typing.js`.
- Records created: patient "Fatima UXF3560" (route A, auto-booked with Dr. Shravya on 08 Nov 2026 09:00-09:30) and "Mohammed Imran UXF4614" (route B, booked 09 Nov 12:30).
- The run was at 18:37 IST, after Dr. Shravya's hours. Route A therefore booked the **next day**, and route B used a date step that is **not** counted below.
- Related walker run: `docs/ux-walker/stories/patients-records--STORY-201/walk-report.md` (the Patients list the walk-in lands on). Visual findings: `docs/ux-visual/pages/dashboard-and-patients-findings.md` (P-xx) and `appointments-and-visits-findings.md` (APT-xx).

**Verdict**: **convoluted**. Two divergent routes produce different records for one goal. Route A's dialog fails 4 screen heuristics, and its **Create button cannot be reached with a mouse at 1280x800**. Route B's auto-opening dialog captures keystrokes meant for the search box.

Labels: **[measured]** means counted live or read from a screenshot or the code. **[suspected]** means a live check is still needed. **[story]** means taken from the catalog and not re-measured.

## The Simpler Version

**One route.** The scheduler is canonical because the walk-in's question is "when can I be seen?". The Patients page gets a shortcut into the same flow instead of its own booking engine.

1. On **Appointments** (doctor remembered, today), she types the walk-in's phone or name into **Find or add patient**.
2. No match: an inline row "+ Add new patient '98850 00111'" is highlighted. Nothing opens by itself. She presses Enter or clicks it. A compact dialog opens with **digits in Phone and letters in Name**, so nothing is re-typed.
3. She fills the missing field (name or phone) and picks Gender (Male / Female / Other as one-click segments, no default).
4. She clicks **Save & book next free: 11:30 today** (primary; the button names the slot it will take), or **Save & pick a slot** (secondary). Either way the booking lands on the grid she is looking at, highlighted, with "Undo".

**4 steps**, against route A's 7 (+3 to see the slot) and route B's 9. She sees the slot before committing.

From **Patients → New Patient**, the same compact form appears, with the optional fields under "More details". Its footer offers **Save** and **Save & book…**, which opens the scheduler with the patient pre-selected. This needs BRK-02 fixed. It also removes the four walk-in fields from the patient form.

### Concrete changes
1. **Remove the walk-in section from Create/Edit Patient** (Patient Type, Doctor (for walk-in), Appointment Type (walk-in), Room) and replace it with a "Save & book…" footer action into the scheduler. That is 4 fields and 1 needless decision gone, one booking engine instead of two, and no silent auto-booking. *Effort M.*
2. **Add "Book next free slot" to the scheduler.** A button on the grid toolbar, or in the post-create step, that **names** the slot before booking ("Next free: 11:30 today"). It keeps route A's one-click convenience, but visible and undoable. *Effort S–M.*
3. **Stop auto-opening quick-create.** Keep the existing "+ Add '…' as a new patient" row as the only trigger, and route digits to Phone and text to Name. This removes the keystroke capture and the re-entry. *Effort S.*
4. **Make Create reachable.** Use a sticky dialog footer, or inner scroll with max-height (P03). Collapse optional fields under "More details" so the form fits at 1280x800. *Effort S.*
5. **Gender with no default** (or segmented buttons), in both dialogs (P05). *Effort S.*
6. **One success pattern.** After any create-and-book: a banner on the grid with "Open patient · Start visit · Undo", not a toast on an unrelated page. *Effort S.*
7. **Write `source: WALK_IN` from whichever route books a same-day new patient**, or drop the field. Today it is written by one route and read by none. *Effort S.*

## Clarity Issues
- **"Patient Type: Walk-in / Non walk-in"** sounds like a stored property of the patient. It is not persisted (create payload in `PatientsManagement.tsx` ~465-478). It is really "book now?". In the Edit dialog the same control re-books an existing patient [story Variation].
- **Two doctor pickers in one form**: "Primary Doctor (Automatic)" and "Doctor (for walk-in)" (`J2:07-A`). Nothing says how they differ apart from paragraphs of help text.
- **Three "Type" fields**: Consultation Type, Patient Type and Appointment Type (walk-in) (`J2:07-A`).
- **Naming drift for one action**: "New Patient" (button) → "Create Patient" (dialog title) → "Create" (submit) on Patients, and "Add New Patient" (dialog) → "Create Patient" (submit) → "+ Add '…' as a new patient" on Appointments. The Quick Guide says "Add Patient", which matches no button (P25).
- **Required markers differ**: the quick-create dialog marks First Name, Gender and Phone with "*" (`J2:02-B`). The Create Patient dialog marks nothing, yet still rejects an empty name or phone (P04, `J2:02-A`).
- **Phone hint is misleading**: the quick-create placeholder suggests "+1234567890 or 9876543210". The full form has no hint at all, and rejects spaces and a leading 0 [STORY-202 Edge Cases].
- **"Walk-in appointment booked — Appointment scheduled for 08 Nov 2026 at 09:00-09:30"** (`J2:09-A`) does not say which doctor, and does not flag that "next available" turned out to be **tomorrow**.
- **Developer-voice copy**: "Capture minimal details to create a patient record without leaving the scheduling flow." (`J2:01-B`); "If Patient Type is Walk-in and a doctor is selected, the next available slot will be automatically booked after saving." (`J2:02-A`).

### Functional bugs that shape this flow (one line each, already in defects.md)
- P03 (ux-visual): the Create Patient dialog overflows the viewport, so route A cannot be finished with a mouse at 1280x800.
- BRK-35: walk-in auto-booking swallows errors, so "No slots available" can be false, and one room conflict skips a whole day.
- BRK-02: "Book appointment" from the chart loses the patient. This blocks the obvious "register first, then book" path that would replace route A.
- BRK-12: after route A the chart says "Next appointment: Not scheduled" while the tracker says "Upcoming appt" [story step 8].
