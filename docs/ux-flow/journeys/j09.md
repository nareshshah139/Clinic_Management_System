# j09: Doctor looks up a patient's last visit, then corrects his phone number

Story: `PATIENT-RECORDS-03` (docs/ux-paths/topics/patient-records.md). Script: `scripts/ux/journeys/j09-patient-lookup.cjs`.
Persona: Dr. Priya Menon (doctor). Patient: Rohan Kulkarni (`ux-patient-2`). He phones and asks what he was given last time and when to come back; he also says his number changed to 9822099102.

Fresh-seed result: `PASS j09 actions=10 decisions=1 reentry=1 screens=9`. Screenshots: `output/ux-journeys/j09-check/j09/`.

Route scripted: the "My Patients" card on `/dashboard` (a doctor's landing page lists her own patients with a one-click row). The route the header pushes is described under "Routes compared" below.

Seed note (measured): Rohan has two completed visits (today's chemical-peel follow-up and 29 Sep) and **no prescription at all**. So "what was he prescribed" has the true answer "nothing recorded", and the goal check asserts on date, complaint, diagnosis and the review advice instead. How the app shows that absence is part of the critique.

### Hidden bug (measured, worth a line)
Saving the Edit dialog with only the phone changed **rewrites the date of birth**. Before: `dob 1988-07-19`. After (`GET /patients/ux-patient-2`): `dob 1987-12-31T18:30Z` (1 Jan 1988 IST). Cause: the dialog always sends `age` (`PatientsManagement.tsx:480`), and the service replaces dob with `ageToApproximateDob(age)` = 1 January (`backend/src/modules/patients/patients.service.ts:67-69, 357`). A one-field correction silently destroys real data.

## 2. Take-away Pass

**`/dashboard` (01-dashboard.png)**
- Recent Visits card already answers two of Rohan's three questions: "Rohan Kulkarni / Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation / 13 Nov 2026, 09:30 am" with zero clicks. But the row opens the editable visit form (`DoctorPatientPanels.tsx:81`), not a read-only summary, and shows no medicines or review date.
- Primary action: none; two cards of equal weight plus a header search. For this task the My Patients row is the right one but Rohan is below the fold; the search box on that card is the faster way to him (2 actions) and it is not the one a person sees first.
- Stated twice: patient identity appears in Recent Visits, My Patients, Today's Appointments (three lists of the same people).

**Patient page, Info tab (02)**
- Remove: the Info tab as the default. It repeats the header card: phone shown in "Primary contact" and again as "Phone:", gender and age shown in the title badges and again in "Gender: M / Age: 38 yrs", email dash twice. Internal "ID: ux-patient-2" badge beside "Code: UX002": a doctor needs only the code.
- Remove or defer: the six-step progress tracker with Continue / Prescribe / Bill buttons (Start, Document, Complete, Prescribe, Billing, Follow-up). It answers "where is today's visit", not "what did I give him". Four buttons in the header (Resume last visit, Start new visit, Book appointment, Back) plus three in the tracker = seven starting points on a read-only look-up.
- Primary action: "Start new visit" is dark and loudest; for a phone enquiry it is the wrong thing to emphasise.
- Last-visit date is stated in the header card (13 Nov 2026), in the History group heading and again in the visit card heading (`03`): three times in one viewport.

**Patient page, History tab (03, 04)**
- Keep: the collapsed card shows date, doctor, complaint and diagnosis well. This is the right content.
- Defer: the filters row (doctor dropdown, two blank date inputs, Clear filters) and "Refresh" sit above a list of two visits. A full-width "Refresh" button is louder than the data.
- Missing: the answer to "medicines" and "when to come back". Expanded card (04) shows Complaint details (repeats the Chief Complaint just above it), Diagnosis details (repeats Diagnosis), Treatment plan "Review in 3 weeks. Sun protection advised.", Vitals. For a visit with no prescription there is no "No medicines prescribed" line, so absence cannot be told from "not loaded". `PatientHistoryVisitCard.tsx:376` renders the Medicines section only when items exist (suspected from reading the code and from `04`, which has no Medicines block).
- "Next appointment: Not scheduled" in the header is the real "when should he come back" field and is the one nearest to the patient's question; the review advice is in the plan notes instead. Two sources for one answer.

**Patients list (05, 06)**
- Three headings for one list: "Patients Management", "Patients", "Patient Records".
- Search box is not focused on arrival (action 5 exists only to focus it).
- Row actions: View, Start Visit, More, all crushed at 1366 px; Edit is two levels deep (More, then Edit).

**Edit Patient dialog (08)**
- One field needed, 14 shown: First Name, Last Name, Age, Gender, Phone, ABHA ID (with helper), Email, Address, Emergency Contact, Consultation Type, Primary Doctor, How did the patient hear about us, Patient Type, Doctor (for walk-in) (with helper). Walk-in booking controls appear on an edit of an existing patient and are meaningless there.
- Name: the seeded full name sits entirely in "First Name" with "Last Name" empty (`08`), so the form misrepresents the record (data-shape quirk, suspected to come from seeding rather than normal creation).
- No success confirmation (09).

## 3. The Simpler Version

1. On `/dashboard`, the doctor types two letters into the My Patients search (or the header search) and clicks Rohan; the header search result goes to his record, not an empty list.
2. The patient page opens on a "Last visit" panel: date, complaint, diagnosis, **Medicines (or "No medicines prescribed")**, **Review: 3 weeks**. Nothing to expand.
3. She clicks the phone number in the contact card; it becomes an input in place.
4. She types 9822099102 and presses Enter. A toast says "Phone updated"; the card shows the new number. Date of birth is untouched.

That is 4 to 5 actions (1 click if Rohan is on screen, +2 for typing a search), against 10.

## 4. Clarity Issues

- "My Patients" means patients whose primary doctor is the signed-in doctor, but nothing on the card says other patients exist. A patient who is not hers is absent here, and the doctor must know to use the Patients page (story variation, `patient-records.md:108`).
- The same list is "Patients" in the sidebar, "Patients Management" as the page title, "Patients" again as a section heading, and "Patient Records" as the card title.
- "Primary contact" is just a phone number; "Primary doctor" sits inside the same card under an email dash.
- "Resume last visit" (header) and "Resume session" (on a visit card) both reopen the editable visit form; a doctor reading history may press them by accident and open a completed visit for editing.
- "Show details" / "Full details" / "Hide details": it is unclear which one reveals medicines.
- History date inputs show `mm/dd/yyyy` while the clinic and the cards use "13 Nov 2026" (`03`).
- "ID:" in the list is a clipped fragment ("ID: atient-2", `05`).

## Routes compared

- **Dashboard My Patients card (scripted)**: 1 click when the patient is on screen, but Rohan is row 9 of 10, below the fold; only doctors' own patients appear.
- **Header search**: type "Rohan" (1 typed action), the dropdown shows phone and age, which is a good summary (`hs.png` in an exploratory run), but clicking the Patient result lands on the Patients list with the internal id in the search box and "No patients found". Measured. Net: a dead end on the route the screen pushes hardest.
- **Patients sidebar, search, row**: 3 actions to the record (4 with the focus click), the same 2-click tail (History, Show details). Used here for the edit half.
- **Recent Visits row**: 0 clicks to read date and diagnosis (screenshot `01`), 1 click opens the editable visit form.

Clicks between "I know the patient's name" and "I can read the last prescription": **3** (patient row, History tab, Show details), and for Rohan the prescription is not there at all. Surfaces repeating identity: phone appears on 6 surfaces (header search, My Patients row, header card, Info tab, list row, Edit dialog); last-visit date on 5 (Recent Visits, My Patients row, header card, History group heading, visit card heading). Edit form: 14 fields for a 1-field change.

## 7. Verdict

**Convoluted.** Ten actions against an ideal of four; the look-up half costs 3 clicks to reach clinical text that could be on the first screen, the edit half sends the doctor out of the record, through a two-level menu into a 14-field dialog, and a one-field phone correction silently overwrites the patient's date of birth. The most prominent route (header search) ends at an empty list.
