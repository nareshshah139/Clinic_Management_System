# Redundancy and information architecture

Cross-journey critique for ClinicMS: duplicate paths, duplicate facts, overlapping features, menu structure, dead surfaces and standing explanations. Written for the owner's goal: an app that clinic staff who are not computer people can use without thinking, with fewer helper lines.

Scope and method
- Inputs: `docs/ux-paths/discovery.md` (Redundancy leads), `docs/ux-paths/catalog.md` (Redundancy Candidates, Core journeys), the journey critiques `docs/ux-flow/journeys/j01, j02, j03, j05, j06, j08, j09, j10.md`. Where a journey critic already proposed a fix, this file cites it and adds only what crosses journeys.
- Live app: every route in the brief was opened as the role that uses it, at 1366x850, with a screenshot and a text dump of the page. Screenshots live in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Clinic-Management-System/922a752e-0d05-5fdd-b934-22f2a04cd167/scratchpad/redundancy/shots/`, written below as `shots/<persona>-<name>.png` (each has a `.txt` text dump beside it). The `pharmacist-*` shots and `reception-{dash,desk,counter,inv,patients,appts,rooms}` were taken before the stack restart at 09:42 UTC; the rest after it, on the fresh seed. Figures quoted from each are from that run.
- Tags: **[M]** measured on the live app or in a screenshot; **[S]** suspected, read from source only. `FE/` is `frontend/src/`, `BE/` is `backend/src/`.

Counts: 14 duplicate paths, 12 duplicate-information findings, 9 overlapping features, 4 IA findings plus a naming table of 13 concepts, 15 dead or broken surfaces, 27 guides and standing explanations. 22 proposed changes, 7 flagged for the owner.

### 4.2 Findings

- I1 **Rare things at top level**: Test Transcribe (developer page, B1), Fee list (changed a few times a year; its screen is three number fields `shots/owner-fees.txt`), Pharmacy Desk (partner sync, packages, AI assistant: none is a per-patient task), Rooms for RECEPTION and NURSE (a view-only occupancy grid that repeats the appointment rows, `shots/reception-rooms.txt`).
- I2 **Daily things that are missing or deep**: a list of unpaid clinic bills does not exist at any depth (B3). With P7, P8 and O3 applied, the area is left with "Quotations" only.

### 4.3 Proposed menu per role

Each entry is justified by a task the role does every working day. Order is by frequency.

RECEPTION (4)
1. Appointments: book, confirm, move, cancel, bill the consultation (catalog core ranks 1, 2, 5). Landing page (j08).
2. Patients: find a returning patient, register a new one (core rank 10).
3. Dashboard: only if j08's role landing turns it into a work queue; otherwise remove.
4. Daily Collection: count the clinic takings at close. New for this role, owner decision O6.
Removed: Rooms (booking dialog shows room availability), Pharmacy Desk, Pharmacy Counter (cannot bill, B6), Inventory (owner decision O2: some small clinics have reception receive deliveries).

DOCTOR (4)
1. Appointments: open the next patient (core rank 3; j03 makes the calendar the doctor's queue).
2. Patients: look up history (j09).
3. Inventory: approve price/stock edits and new items (the sidebar badge, catalog STORY-050). Doctors are the approvers.
4. Procedures: laser and peel sessions with machine data, several a day in a dermatology clinic.
Removed: Visits (reached from the appointment row, patient page and dashboard; the setup page stays at its URL), Rooms, Pharmacy Desk, Pharmacy Counter, Sales, Pharmacy Report, Daily Collection, Test Transcribe. An owner-doctor who also runs the money uses the OWNER menu.

PHARMACIST (5)
1. Pharmacy Counter: prescriptions waiting, dispense, bill (core rank 6). Landing page.
2. Sales: walk-in sale, reprint, discount, refund, returns (core rank 11).
3. Inventory: receive bills, low stock, counts.
4. Daily Collection: drawer count at close.
5. Reports: GST and margin (the merged entry, F2).
Removed: Dashboard (admin stat cards only, `shots/pharmacist-dash.png`), Pharmacy Desk.

OWNER (9)
1. Dashboard: alerts as links, today's numbers.
2. Daily Collection: whole-day takings.
3. Reports: merged entry (F2).
4. Appointments, 5. Patients: oversight.
6. Sales, 7. Inventory: pharmacy oversight and approvals.
8. Users: staff, holidays, hours; the Fee list opens from a "Fees" button in its header, the same pattern as the existing "Clinic Holidays" button (`UsersManagement.tsx`, header).
9. Rooms (Manage Rooms tab): setup, kept for the owner only.
Removed: Test Transcribe, Fee list (moved under Users), Pharmacy Report (merged), Visits and Procedures (reached from patients and appointments; keep for OWNER if the owner is also the treating doctor, which the role table cannot tell).

NURSE keeps Appointments, Patients, Visits (vitals and photos from the visit form). MANAGER and ACCOUNTANT follow OWNER minus Users and Fees and Daily Collection plus Reports respectively; no new evidence for them on the live app, so no further change.
