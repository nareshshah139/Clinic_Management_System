One morning, one patient

{{returningName}} is booked at the desk, seen by Dr. Shravya, and collects her medicines at the pharmacy counter. Nobody walks a paper slip anywhere. Each hand-off below happens because someone saved something.

Read the map like this

Solid box: reception. Grey band: the doctor's consult. Dashed box: pharmacy. The black box, lesson 7, is the only step that sends anything to another person, and it happens when the doctor presses Create Prescription.

The one trap

The dashed arrow from 6 to 8 is a shortcut you must not take. A template fills the examination notes, and that alone switches Complete Visit on. Complete it then and the visit closes with no prescription, so the pharmacy counter has nothing for the patient. Always see Sent to pharmacy queue first.

Read the doctor's day

The calendar now stretches to fit every booking of the day. Anything outside the doctor's working hours sits in striped rows, and a badge at the top counts them.

Do this

  1. Open Appointments. Choose the Doctor 1 and the Date 2.
  2. Read the badge 3. 1 outside working hours means one booking is before opening or after closing.
  3. Scroll to the striped rows 5 to find it. Today it is Rohit Agarwal at 18:30 4.

Doctor Calendar Click a free slot to book an appointment. {{outsideBadge}}

New since last time

One calendar instead of two tabs, and the 18:30 patient is on it. Before this update, a booking like his was saved but not drawn.

Rohit Agarwal arrives at 18:30 and nobody at the desk can find him, because everyone looked at 09:00 to 18:00 only.

Reception Appointments

Book a returning patient

A free slot is the start button. Click it and the booking box opens with the cursor in the patient search, so you pick the time first and the person second.

Do this

  1. Click {{returningName}}'s free {{slotReturning}} row 1. Book Appointment opens.
  2. Just type. The cursor is already in the search 2. Type Meghana or her phone, then click her name 3.
  3. Check the summary and press Confirm Booking 4, fixed at the bottom of the box.

New since last time

Lesson 1 showed you the day. Now you put {{returningName}} into it, starting from the slot.

If you skip it

Look for a Patient box on the calendar before clicking a slot and you will not find one. It moved into the booking box. Always start with the slot.

Part A · ReceptionLesson 3 of 9
3
Reception Appointments

Add a new patient while booking

When the search finds nobody, you register the patient from inside the booking box. The phone you typed carries over, and you land back in the same booking with the new patient chosen.

Do this

  1. Click the free 15:00 slot. Type the phone in the search 1. When it says no match, press Add new patient 2.
  2. Check the phone 3. Fill First Name 4, Last Name and Gender. Press Create Patient 5.
  3. Back in the booking, check his name 6. Press Confirm Booking, as in lesson 2.

Words on screen

No matching patients. Add new patient Add New Patient Create Patient

New since last time

Lesson 2 needed the patient to exist already. Now a walk-in is registered and booked without leaving the calendar.

If you skip it

Search by name only and you can miss a patient registered under another spelling, then create a second record for the same person. Search by phone first. Add new only when the phone finds nobody.

Fill the prescription from a template

A template writes a whole prescription in one click: medicines, examination notes and its own diagnosis. It writes over what is already in those fields, so read the diagnosis again afterwards.

Do this

  1. In the Prescription tab, open Templates 1, where it says No template.
  2. Choose the template that fits 2. For Meghana it is {{templateName}}. Medicines and examination notes fill in.
  3. Scroll back to Diagnosis and read it. Correct it if the template replaced yours.

Seen on the test system

Not saved yet

Part B · Doctor, then PharmacyLesson 7 of 9
7
DoctorPharmacy Visit · Pharmacy Counter

Saving the prescription sends it

Pressing Create Prescription is the hand-off. The prescription goes into the pharmacy queue at that moment, and you stay on the visit. There is no "Go to Pharmacy" step any more.

Do this

  1. Scroll to the end of the prescription. Press Create Prescription 1.
  2. Look for Sent to pharmacy queue 2 next to the item total. That badge is your proof.
  3. Stay on the visit. Send the patient to the counter; the prescription is already there 3.

New since last time

Until now everything stayed in the consult room. Saving puts it in front of the pharmacist.

If you skip it

Press Complete Visit without saving and the visit closes with no prescription. The patient reaches the counter and the queue has nothing for her. This was tried on the test system: a template was chosen, nothing was saved, and the visit completed with no prescription at all.

Part B · DoctorLesson 8 of 9
8
Doctor Visit

Complete the visit

A visit can be completed once it has a diagnosis and either a saved prescription or examination notes. It checks the saved record, so a visit you reopen later is still ready.

Do this

  1. Check you can see Sent to pharmacy queue (lesson 7).
  2. Scroll to the footer. It should name nothing missing.
  3. Press Complete Visit 1 and wait for Visit completed.

What you no longer need

Photos, lab tests and vitals are optional. A scalp consult with no photos can be completed. Ignore the small N of 5 sections complete count in the footer. It still counts tabs, and it does not decide anything. After reopening this visit it read 0 of 5 while Complete Visit was ready.

Words on screen

Complete Visit Visit completed All documentation has been marked complete.

New since last time

{{returningName}}'s consult is closed, with her prescription waiting at the counter.

If you skip it

The visit stays open in your list. At the end of the day nobody can tell which patients were seen and which are still waiting.
